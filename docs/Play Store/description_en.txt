How well do you know the world? Flags Game - Quiz turns 197 countries, flags, maps, capitals, currencies, and coats of arms into quick, colorful geography games.

SEVEN WAYS TO PLAY
• Flag Puzzles: spell the country shown by its flag.
• Country Puzzles: recognize a country from its map.
• Coat of Arms Puzzles: identify national emblems.
• Find Flag: choose the country that matches the flag.
• Match Flags: match a country name to the correct flag.
• Which Country: identify the highlighted country on the map.
• Daily Challenge: race through 10 mixed questions against the clock.

Work through the levels at your own pace, chase longer streaks in the three quick games, or compete for the fastest Daily Challenge time.

CHOOSE YOUR PART OF THE WORLD
Play with all 197 countries or focus on Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania, or South America. Each continent keeps its own record, so every region offers a fresh challenge.

USE HINTS WHEN YOU NEED THEM
Reveal a correct letter, clear wrong letters, finish a difficult puzzle, or remove half of the answers. Hints make learning easier while staying completely optional when you want a tougher geography quiz.

COMPETE WITH GOOGLE PLAY GAMES
Unlock 23 achievements, compare your best All-filter streaks on three global leaderboards, and race on the daily timed leaderboard. Sign-in is optional, and the core geography games can be played offline.

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
Explore country names, capitals, currencies, flags, maps, and coats of arms for 197 countries. Play in English, Danish, German, Spanish, French, Norwegian, Brazilian Portuguese, European Portuguese, or Swedish.

Made for geography fans, students, travelers, families, and anyone who enjoys flag quizzes, map games, country trivia, and learning about the world.
